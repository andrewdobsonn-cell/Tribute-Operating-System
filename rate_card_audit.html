Rate-Card Audit

Caregiver pay-rate governance — snapshot · active caregivers across markets. Derived from live Viv rate rules.
⚠ Gross Rate Anomalies
Caregivers whose rate for a single visit type is far out of line with their market (>12%), while their other rates sit at market. These are the high-confidence likely errors (or undocumented exceptions) — the ones worth fixing first.

📋 Candidate Rate Card
The most common (modal) pay rate per market & visit type — the system's best guess at your standard rate. Confirm or correct each rate. Once locked in, every caregiver whose rate differs can be flagged automatically (that's what makes the $0.50-type errors catchable). "Coverage" = share of caregivers already at that rate; low coverage or many alternates = a rate worth a closer look.
This is a starting point, not the source of truth yet. Legitimate variation (tenure/negotiated raises) is mixed in — edit the standard where it's wrong, then export. A future version can add tenure tiers.
